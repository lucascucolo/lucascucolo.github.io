### Hey, I'm Lucas Cucolo 👋

By day, I work as an **SRE (Site Reliability Engineer)**. Years ago, though, I was just a kid dreaming of being a rockstar in a metal band who couldn't care less about grammar or learning English.

Life happened, priorities shifted, and I built a career in tech and infrastructure. But while I love technology and keeping complex systems reliable, I realized I didn't want my entire universe to revolve around servers, incidents, metrics, and technical jargon.

There is another side to everything—**That One Side** we often overlook: the simple, messy, beautiful side called *life*.

---

### Why this blog exists

In a world where AI can write an entire essay in three seconds, I created this space for something genuinely human:
- **Practicing My English:** Writing raw, honest thoughts by myself, learning through iteration, and improving with every post.
- **Life Beyond Tech:** Stepping out of the SRE bubble to share reflections, habits, books, and thoughts on the world.
- **Curiosity & Growth:** Documenting what catches my attention—whether it’s a new mindset, a life lesson, or just something fun.

Thanks for stopping by. Feel free to explore the posts, and if something resonates with you, reach out at [lucas.cucolo@gmail.com](mailto:lucas.cucolo@gmail.com) or check out the [Contact]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) page!

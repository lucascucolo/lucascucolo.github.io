Welcome to my new blog! As a software engineer and continuous improvement enthusiast, I decided to apply concepts like the PDCA cycle to my daily routine and my English learning journey.

## Why English and Processes?

Writing in English used to feel like a bottleneck. By treating language learning as an iterative process—just like fixing technical debt—I aim to measure progress, eliminate friction, and share what I learn along the way.

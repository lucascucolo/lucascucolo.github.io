Have you ever thought: Why we need to learn math or find the "x" in high school?
I don't know in your country, but here in Brazil, it is the most common complaint among teenagers. No one here has a good motivation to explain why this is so important, finding the "x" is just important to finish school nothing more. We don't think about every thing that you learn in the process. Every skill that you need to read, understand the context, find values that help you to find. 

But all of this it's OK, when we are young we don't have a lot of perspective on life, and we don't have experience to understand things to improve, in this moment of our life we are not too rational, things like intense moments with friends are something that motivates us.

In the article "How the brain makes decisions and how the deliberative system is important to rational decisions"(by Phillip Geraldo de Carvalho Souza and Prof. Ramon Cosenza), neuroscience explains that the brain has two processing systems.
 * Type 1 (Automatic/Emotional): Fast, instinctive and guided by immediate rewards and emotions
 * Type 2 (deliberative/Rational): Slow, reflexive, conscious and dependent on the prefrontal cortex

I was a teenager good at logic, my father always showed me a lot of puzzles just for fun. and I think this maybe helped me to have a good relation with numbers and logic, but when we look at the other side of school, in that moment I didn't want to look at anything about letters and, English, geography, history... 

And it's Ok too at that moment, now I know that life is very flexible, much more than we think, we are always being afraid of everything, because we feel fear of the unknown.

Now, I can understand how important geography, history and English are. It's like my conceptions, my mindset shifted, and I'm a new person, hahaha.

Thank God for understanding logic early, because now I just want to read and write, haha.

Just to finish this post, without any conclusion, I hope at least you can understand that it is never too late to learn, because everything is tomorrow ;)
